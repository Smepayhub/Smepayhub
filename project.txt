SMEHUB MVP – Project Brief
Overview
SMEHUB is a business payment platform designed to help small and medium-sized businesses accept and track digital payments through Stellar.
The MVP focuses on a simple merchant payment acceptance experience.
Sprint Goal
Build a functional Stellar Testnet merchant payment platform where businesses can generate payment requests, receive USDC Testnet payments, and track transactions.
Core Features
1. Merchant Registration
Create a business profile
Connect a Stellar Testnet wallet
Configure business payment details
View merchant wallet information
2. Merchant Dashboard
View payment activity
Track completed and pending payments
View transaction history
Access individual payment records
3. Payment Request Generation
Enter payment amount
Select USDC Testnet
Generate a payment request
Display a payment QR code or payment link
4. Customer Checkout
Open a merchant payment request
Review merchant and amount
Connect a Stellar wallet
Approve the payment
Receive payment confirmation
5. Transaction Tracking
Monitor payment status
Match payments to requests
Display successful and failed transactions
Provide Stellar Testnet transaction hashes
